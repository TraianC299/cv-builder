Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are commonly used by recruiters and employers to filter out CVs during the job application process. By following these tips, you can optimize your CV for ATS and increase your chances of making it to the interview stage:

1. **Use ATS-friendly file types:** Most ATS prefer PDF or Word (.docx) file types. Make sure your CV is saved in one of these formats before submitting.

2. **Include relevant keywords:** ATS scans for job-specific keywords. Tailor your CV for each job application and include keywords from the job description.

3. **Avoid fancy formatting and graphics:** ATS can struggle with complex designs, tables, and graphics. Stick to simple, clean formatting for best results.

4. **Use standard section headings:** ATS recognize standard headings such as "Experience", "Education", and "Skills". Avoid creative headings to ensure your information is properly categorized.

5. **Don't forget to proofread:** Even though ATS are machines, human eyes will eventually read your CV. Avoid spelling and grammatical errors to make a good impression.

By following these tips, you can improve your CV's chances of passing through ATS filters and landing in the hands of a human recruiter. Remember, the goal of your CV is to get you an interview, so make sure it's optimized to help you achieve that.
