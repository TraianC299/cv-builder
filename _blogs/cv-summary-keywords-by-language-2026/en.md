The summary at the top of your CV is one of the highest-impact sections for both recruiters and ATS.

Current search behavior shows strong intent around phrases like **resume summary examples**, **resume summary examples with no experience**, and **resume summary for students**.

## English Search Terms to Target

- resume summary examples
- resume summary examples with no experience
- resume summary examples for students
- resume summary examples for customer service
- professional profile resume

## How to Write an ATS-Friendly Summary

Keep it to 3-4 lines and include:

- target role
- years of experience (or training focus)
- key tools/skills
- measurable impact

Template:

`[Role] with [X years / training background], skilled in [tools/skills], known for [impact/result].`

## Example (No Experience)

`Entry-level marketing graduate with internship and project experience in social media planning, Canva, and analytics reporting. Built a university campaign that increased event registrations by 28%.`

## Final Tip

A strong summary is specific, keyword-aligned, and short. Match the job description language and update it for every application.
