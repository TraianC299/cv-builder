When recruiters scan your CV, the **Work Experience** section is often where they pause the longest.

Whether you have 10 years of experience or are fresh out of school, the way you **structure and phrase your job history** can be the difference between getting an interview or being ignored.

This 2025 guide will show you how to **write, format, and optimize** your work experience like a pro.

---

## ✅ What Counts as Work Experience?

Work experience doesn’t just mean full-time jobs. You can include:
- Part-time jobs
- Internships
- Freelance work
- Volunteering (if relevant)
- Personal projects (especially in tech or design)
- Entrepreneurship or side hustles

The key is **relevance** and **impact**.

---

## 📐 Ideal Structure for Each Entry

Each job or experience should follow this format:

**Job Title** – *Company Name* | City, Country  
*Start Date – End Date*  
- Bullet point 1: what you did + how you did it + result  
- Bullet point 2: a key task or achievement  
- Bullet point 3: something quantifiable if possible

✅ **Tip**: Use **reverse chronological order** (most recent first).

---

## ✍️ How to Write Effective Bullet Points

Bad:  
> Responsible for managing social media  
Good:  
> Created and scheduled 3 weekly campaigns, increasing engagement by 45% in 6 months

Bad:  
> Helped in customer service  
Good:  
> Assisted 30+ customers daily, maintaining a 98% satisfaction rate over 12 months

### 📌 Formula:  
**[Action Verb] + [Task/Responsibility] + [Result/Metric/Tool]**

Use verbs like: *Managed*, *Designed*, *Implemented*, *Optimized*, *Launched*, *Resolved*, *Collaborated*, *Analyzed*…

---

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Listing responsibilities only — instead, show **achievements**
- ❌ Using vague language like “various tasks” or “assisted”
- ❌ Copy-pasting job descriptions
- ❌ Writing in paragraphs instead of bullet points
- ❌ Not tailoring to the job you're applying for

*Sep 2020 – Dec 2022*

---

## 🧠 What if You Have No Experience?

Focus on:
- Internships  
- University or group projects  
- Volunteering  
- Online courses + applied skills  
- Transferable skills (teamwork, time management, problem-solving)

> Use the same bullet format to describe your contributions and results — even in academic or unpaid contexts.
