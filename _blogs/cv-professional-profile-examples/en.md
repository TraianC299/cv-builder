If your CV starts with “My name is…” or “I’m a hard-working, motivated individual…”, it’s time to rethink your strategy.

The **professional profile** (also called a *personal statement* or *summary*) is the very first section recruiters see — and it has **5–7 seconds** to grab their attention.

---

## ✅ What Is a Professional Profile in a CV?

A professional profile is a **short paragraph** (3–5 lines) placed at the top of your CV, right after your contact info.  
It summarizes **who you are professionally**, what you bring to the table, and what you’re looking for in your career.

---

## 🧠 What Should It Include?

A strong professional profile answers four key questions:

- What experience do you have?
- What are your core skills?
- What results have you achieved?
- What kind of role are you seeking?

---

## 📌 Writing Tips

1. **Avoid generic buzzwords**  
   Skip vague terms like “motivated,” “hard-working,” or “dynamic.” Be specific.

2. **Keep it concise and professional**  
   Don’t exceed 4–5 lines. Recruiters scan quickly.

3. **Include numbers or results if possible**  
   Anything measurable (e.g., “5 years’ experience”, “increased revenue by 20%”) adds weight.

4. **Tailor it to the job**  
   A profile for marketing should look different from one in IT, sales, or education.

---

## ✍️ Real Examples by Industry

**✅ Marketing**  
> Digital marketing specialist with over 3 years of experience in performance and social media campaigns. Increased organic traffic by 65% for B2C clients. Seeking to scale data-driven strategies within a dynamic team.

**✅ IT / Development**  
> Front-end developer with 2 years of experience in React and TypeScript. Delivered 6 fully responsive web apps for European startups. Looking to contribute to high-performance projects in a collaborative environment.

**✅ Sales**  
> B2B sales representative with a proven track record in lead generation and closing (€250,000 in new contracts in 2024). Seeking to help businesses grow their client base in a results-driven role.

**✅ No Experience (Student)**  
> Computer Science graduate passionate about AI and software development. Participated in hackathons and contributed to open-source projects. Looking for an entry-level role with learning opportunities and hands-on challenges.
