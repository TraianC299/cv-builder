## 1. The Critical Rule: File Size

Recruiters and ATS platforms often have **strict upload limits**. The larger the file, the slower it uploads and the more likely it is to be flagged or discarded.

The key takeaway is simple: **Keep your CV PDF file size under 1MB.**

- **Under 500KB:** Ideal and safe for any system.
- **500KB - 1MB:** Acceptable, but approaching the limit.
- **Over 1MB:** **Risky.** Files this large usually contain unnecessary, high-resolution images or poorly compressed graphics.

> **Tip:** If your file is too big, check your embedded images (logos, portfolio shots). Downsize them before generating the PDF.

---

## 2. Font Choice: Readability vs. Reliability

While a fancy font might seem unique, a non-standard font can render incorrectly on different systems, especially within an ATS.

### Recommended (ATS-Safe) Fonts:

- **Serif:** Times New Roman, Georgia (excellent for printed readability)
- **Sans-Serif:** **Arial, Calibri, Verdana** (best for digital screens and ATS)

### Fonts to Avoid:

- **Rare or Custom Fonts:** If the recipient's computer doesn't have the font installed, it defaults to a basic, ugly, or unreadable substitute.
- **Decorative Fonts:** They look unprofessional and decrease readability.

---

## 3. Formatting & Structure for the ATS

The Applicant Tracking System (ATS) isn't interested in design; it's interested in **data extraction**. If your CV uses elements it can't read, you get screened out.

Here is what the ATS loves:

- **Plain Text Layer:** Ensure your PDF has a clean, selectable text layer (i.e., you can highlight the text with your cursor). PDFs created from scanned images are invisible to the ATS.
- **Simple Headers:** Use standard headings (e.g., "Work Experience," "Education") and avoid putting critical data inside complex tables or text boxes.
- **No Macros or Forms:** Never embed interactive elements or forms within your CV PDF.

## 4. Professional File Naming

Sending a file named `CV_final_final_v3.pdf` is unprofessional. Treat the file name as part of your application package.

**The Golden Rule for Naming:**
**`FirstName_LastName_CV_TargetRole.pdf`**

**Example:** `John_Smith_CV_SoftwareEngineer.pdf`

This makes your file easy to search, categorize, and identify, showing attention to detail.
